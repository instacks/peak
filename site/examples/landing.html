Questions

Can I try it without a card?

Yes. The Starter plan is free for up to three people, for as long as you like.

Where are my notes stored?

In data centers in the EU, encrypted at rest. You can export everything as Markdown at any time.

Can guests edit notes?

Guests can read and comment. Give them a seat to let them edit.

What happens when I cancel?

Your space turns read-only for 90 days, so you can export it, and is then deleted.
