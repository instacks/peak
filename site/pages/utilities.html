Text colors

.peak-text-primary, -secondary, -contrast, -accent, -muted, -success, -danger, -info. There's no warning text color: warning is too light for text.

Visually hidden

.peak-visually-hidden hides an element on screen but keeps it for screen readers: a legend for a row of chips, a label for an icon button. It shows again when something inside it has focus.

Scoping

  • .peak and .peak-ignore choose where the scoped build applies.
  • data-peak-scope starts a new set of inputs (Theming).
  • data-peak-anchor anchors a popover or dropdown to its trigger (Menus and popovers).
