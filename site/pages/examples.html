--- title: Examples group: Examples order: 1 wide: true description: Whole pages built with Peak's classes and nothing else, to show how the site chrome, the layouts and the components fit together. ---

Each example is a complete HTML page. Its only CSS is a short <style> block that sets a few inputs on :root: that's the example's theme, shown below each preview. Everything else is Peak. A theme saved in the customizer applies to the examples too, on top of their own.

A preview is a viewport of its own, as wide as this column: on a narrow screen it shows the example's phone layout. Open the full page to see it at your window's size.
