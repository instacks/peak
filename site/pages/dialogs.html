Modal dialog

A <dialog> with an <article> is Blades' modal. Buttons open and close it with command and commandfor (the Invoker Commands API), so no script is needed. closedby="any" also closes it on Esc and a click outside.

The dialog fades in while its card zooms in slightly (from --peak-dialog-scale, 0.96), and fades out when it closes (Chromium keeps it in the top layer while it does; other browsers fade it out underneath). With reduced motion it opens and closes at once; with reduced transparency the backdrop isn't blurred.

Older browsers

command/commandfor and closedby reached the major browsers during 2025, after Peak's baseline. For older browsers, load Peak's peak-commands.js (@instacks/peak/commands, a few lines that do nothing where the browser has both; these docs load it), or call dialog.showModal() and dialog.close() from your own click handler. Esc closes a modal dialog everywhere.

A form in a dialog

method="dialog" closes the dialog when the form is submitted, after the browser checks the required fields. The submit button can sit in the footer with the others: form="…" ties it to the form. Peak keeps it in the footer's row (Pico makes submit buttons full width).

New contact

A button does one thing without script: it closes the dialog or opens a toast, not both. Showing a toast after the form is saved takes a line of script.

Drawer

.peak-drawer is a full-height panel that slides in from the inline end (the right in left-to-right pages); .start uses the other side. Right-to-left pages flip both. --peak-drawer-width sets the width (24rem). The page list of these docs is a drawer on small screens.
