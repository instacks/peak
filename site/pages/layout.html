Sections

Every <section> gets space below it: --peak-section-spacing when set, otherwise Blades' block spacing. .peak-section does the same for elements that can't be a <section>.

:root { --peak-section-spacing: 5rem; }

Band

.peak-band is a section whose background runs to the edges of the viewport while its content stays in the container. No 100vw tricks: no horizontal scrollbar, nothing to flip in right-to-left pages. The default background is the sunken surface.

A sunken band

The background reaches the viewport edges.

Variants:

  • .surface: the surface color (white in light mode).
  • .primary: the primary fill, with light text.
  • .contrast: the contrast color, with inverted text.
  • On both, headings, lead text, eyebrows, links and focus rings take the band's text color. Buttons keep their fill: use .contrast or .secondary buttons on a primary band.
  • [data-theme="dark"] (or light): the page background of that scheme, with everything inside in that scheme.

Without a container around it, a band is as wide as its parent; its padding is --peak-section-spacing.

Hero

.peak-hero: generous space above and below, at most about two lines of h1 wide. .center centers it, its lead and any cluster of buttons inside. Combine it with .peak-display, an eyebrow and .peak-lead.
