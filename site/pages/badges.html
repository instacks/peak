Badges

.peak-badge is a small pill label. Colors: default (primary), .secondary, .contrast, .accent, .success, .warning, .danger, .info. Each pair of text and background is contrast-tested.

Primary Secondary Contrast Accent Success Warning Danger Info

Solid, outline and dot

.solid uses the filled color, .outline drops the background, .dot adds a status dot.

In text and headings

A badge sizes itself relative to the surrounding text. A label too long for its container wraps inside it, with rounded corners instead of the pill ends.

Chips

.peak-chip is larger than a badge and meant to be interacted with. As a link, it's a tag; around a checkbox or radio, it's a filter (the input stays accessible but isn't drawn); with a button inside, it's removable.

Avatars

.peak-avatar is a round picture or initials. Give initials role="img" and a label. --peak-avatar-size sets the size (2.5rem).
