Linked card

.peak-card-link on one link stretches it over the whole card: the card becomes a single click target, lifts on hover and shows the focus ring around itself. Other links and buttons in the card stay clickable on their own.

Image cards

An image, picture or figure that is the card's first child runs to its edges under the rounded corners. An inline SVG doesn't, so an icon at the top of a card stays an icon; wrap an SVG illustration in a <figure> to make it bleed.

Mountains

The image bleeds to the card's edges.

Product

Use <img> or <picture> the same way.

Horizontal card

.peak-card.horizontal puts its leading image beside the content once the card is at least 32rem wide, and above it when narrower. It's a container query on the card itself, so it works the same in a sidebar, a grid or full width.

Horizontal

Beside the image from a card width of 32rem. Make the window narrower and it stacks.

Details

Cards in grids

In .peak-grid-auto and Blades' .grid, cards fill their row's height and push their footers to the bottom. See Auto grid.

Tokens

  • --peak-card-bg: the card background (the surface).
  • --peak-shadow: the card shadow, shared with menus and dialogs.
  • --peak-radius and --peak-spacing set the corners and padding.
