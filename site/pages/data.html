Steps

.peak-steps on an ordered list: numbered circles joined by a line. Mark the current step with aria-current="step"; the steps before it show as done (✓).

  1. Download
    Get the add-on from your account.
  2. Install
    Double-click the file.
  3. Activate
    Enter your license key.

Without aria-current, all steps look alike: a plain numbered sequence.
