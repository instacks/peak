Buttons

<button>, input[type=submit] and links with role="button". Variants: .secondary, .contrast, and .outline with any of them.

Groups

role="group" joins buttons (or an input and a button) into one control.

With a field in the group, the field gives up the room and a <button> keeps its label on one line.

A field fills its line. For a short one beside its label (a quantity, a sort order), put them in a .peak-cluster, where fields keep their own width.

aria-invalid="true" or "false" marks a field, in the danger or success color; a following <small> takes the same color.
