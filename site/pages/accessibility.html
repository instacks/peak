--- title: Accessibility group: Reference order: 3 description: Peak follows the visitor's settings for motion, contrast, transparency and colors, and keeps its color pairs readable by construction. ---

Contrast by construction

Every text color is tested against every background it's used on, for the whole range of every color input, in both schemes: 4.5:1 for text (WCAG 2 AA), 3:1 for large text, controls and focus rings. Exact colors (--peak-*-light, --peak-*-dark) are the only way out of that guarantee.

Focus rings are opaque and --peak-outline-width wide (2px by default). Interactive targets added by Peak (chip remove buttons, pagination links) are at least 24px (WCAG 2.5.8).

Reduced motion

With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, Blades turns off transitions and animations, and Peak adds the cases that rule misses: accordions open at once, drawers don't slide, and linked cards don't lift. --peak-duration is 0s.

More contrast

With prefers-contrast: more (macOS "Increase contrast", Windows contrast themes), muted text and borders become stronger.

Reduced transparency

With prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce, the backdrop behind dialogs isn't blurred.

Forced colors

In forced-colors mode (Windows High Contrast), the system replaces every color and removes shadows. Peak keeps shapes and states visible:

  • Cards, popovers, dialogs, badges and avatars get a border where a shadow drew their shape.
  • Focus rings become outlines in the Highlight color.
  • The current page, selected chips, solid badges and the current step use the system's highlight colors.
  • The open tab and the current sidebar link are marked with Highlight.

Print

  • The light scheme, on white, also for a dark page.
  • No header, sidebar, skip link, menu button, scheme switch, footer links or breadcrumb.
  • No shadows or band backgrounds; cards and callouts get a thin border.
  • Cards, figures, code blocks, quotes and table rows aren't split across pages; headings stay with what follows them.
  • External links in .peak-prose print their address.

Right-to-left

Peak uses logical properties (margin-inline-start, inset-inline-end, position-area: block-end span-inline-end) throughout, so layouts, menus, toasts, callout edges and the sidebar navigation mirror in dir="rtl". Drawers slide in from the correct side.
